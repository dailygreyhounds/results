Closing line value
Did the price we took beat the price the market closed at? bsp ÷ odds — below 1.00 means the dog firmed after we bet (we got the bigger price, positive CLV); above 1.00 means it drifted and the closing market disagreed with us. This matters because the staked book's per-bet SD/mean ratio is 12.6, so ROI needs ~610 settled bets to be two sigma from zero and we have 198. CLV converges much faster — it asks a question of every bet, not just the winners.

Race Simulation
Probabilities are a conditional logit over the field (market price + early speed + draw fit), finishing order sampled Plackett-Luce over 4,000 runs. Traffic risk is now measured, not guessed — built from each dog's own position-in-running history, its rail index, and the true box including vacant-box gaps. Validated on 20,692 runners: it predicts a worse-than-usual jump (p=0.002) but does not beat the market (p=0.34), so read it as what can go wrong, never as a price signal. The animation stays illustrative — there is no trajectory data anywhere in this pipeline.

Multis
A multi compounds the bookmaker's margin as well as any edge — three legs from a 122% book carry an effective overround of 182%, so each leg needs a 22% relative edge just to break even. Only Golden-70 (1.310) and S10 (1.533) clear that and strike often enough to be usable. M2r and Golden+70 do not. M1 clears it on paper (2.531) but wins 21% of the time, so a 3-leg lands about once in a hundred — excluded.
Nothing here is proven. Ten combinations were tested; one reached p<0.05 against 0.5 expected by chance. The largest sample is 48 days. Treat these as a small side bet on qualifying days, not a strategy — and note multis are the quickest way to attract a bookmaker's attention.

Reading the Results Table
📊ROI
Return on Investment. A ROI of +50% means for every $1,500 staked, you returned $2,250 on average. Calculated as (total returns − total staked) ÷ total staked × 100.
📈Running P/L
Cumulative profit or loss from bet one to now. Green means you're ahead of where you started; red means behind. Resets to zero at the beginning of tracking.
🎯Place Rate
How often a selection finishes 1st, 2nd or 3rd. A high place rate with lower win rate indicates a consistent model that's worth watching for each-way opportunities.
📅Frequency
How many selections per day a model generates on average. A model at 0.32/day fires roughly every 3 days. Lower frequency models need more patience but can be higher conviction.
